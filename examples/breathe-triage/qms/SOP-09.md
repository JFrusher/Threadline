# SOP-09 Training

**Purpose.** Ensure personnel are competent (ISO 13485:2016 §6.2).

## Procedure
1. Define competence for each role in `threadline.yaml` signers (author, reviewer, qa, release_manager).
2. Train before a person signs anything; record a `training_record` with `effectiveness` evidence.
3. Before adding a person's key to `qms/signers/allowed_signers`, QA verifies their identity and records it.

## Records
`records/training/`.
