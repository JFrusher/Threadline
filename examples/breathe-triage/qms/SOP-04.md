# SOP-04 CAPA

**Purpose.** Eliminate causes of nonconformities and potential nonconformities (ISO 13485:2016 §8.5.2, §8.5.3).

## Procedure
1. Open a `capa` item linking its `sources` (complaint, management review, vulnerability).
2. Record the `problem`, investigate and record the `root_cause`.
3. Plan the `action`; implement it through normal change control (SOP-02, PR template).
4. Verify the action does not adversely affect safety or performance (risk review, SOP-03).
5. Record the `effectiveness_check` and set `status: effective` when closed.
