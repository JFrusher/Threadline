# SOP-05 Complaints and Vigilance

**Purpose.** Receive, evaluate and report complaints (ISO 13485:2016 §8.2.2, §8.2.3).

## Procedure
1. Record every complaint as a `complaint` item within one working day: `received`, `description`, `markets`.
2. Decide reportability and record `reportability_rationale`.
3. **United Kingdom (Great Britain).** Report serious incidents to the MHRA under Part 4A of the UK MDR 2002
   (inserted by SI 2024/1368, in force 16 June 2025). The general serious-incident deadline is 15 days from
   awareness; shorter deadlines apply to deaths, serious deteriorations and serious public health threats —
   confirm the current figures in MHRA guidance before each report (PRD §11). Northern Ireland follows EU rules.
4. **African markets.** Report to the national regulatory authority of each country under its domesticated law
   (AU Model Law on Medical Products Regulation). Record the national rule applied in the rationale.
5. Feed every complaint into risk management (SOP-03) and, where needed, CAPA (SOP-04).
