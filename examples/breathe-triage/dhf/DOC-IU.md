# Intended use — Breathe-Triage (FICTIONAL)

> Fictional example device for Threadline. Not clinical software.

**Intended purpose.** Breathe-Triage is standalone software running on a handheld Android-class device. It helps
community health workers (CHWs) count a child's respiratory rate and classify fast breathing and general danger
signs in children aged 2 to 59 months, to support the decision to refer, treat per local protocol, or advise home
care.

**Users.** Trained CHWs with basic literacy in the device language. Not for use by caregivers.
**Patients.** Children aged 2–59 months with cough or difficult breathing. Not for infants under 2 months.
**Environment.** Community settings and homes, indoors and outdoors, including bright sunlight and low light, with
no mains power and no network for days at a time.
**Contraindications.** None; use outside the age range is blocked (RC-002).
**Clinical basis.** Age-banded fast-breathing thresholds (WHO Bulletin 2003; see PRD §11).
