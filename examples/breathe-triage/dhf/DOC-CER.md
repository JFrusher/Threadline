# Clinical evaluation — Breathe-Triage (ILLUSTRATIVE, fictional device)

> Illustrative structure only. A real clinical evaluation needs a literature search protocol, appraisal and a
> qualified evaluator. Nothing here is a claim about real patients.

## 1. Scope
Intended use per DOC-IU: classification of fast breathing and danger signs in children 2–59 months by CHWs.

## 2. Clinical background and state of the art
Age-banded respiratory-rate thresholds (≥50/min for 2–11 months, ≥40/min for 12–59 months) were reviewed in the
*Bulletin of the World Health Organization* (2003, 81(4):298–300), which reports the best sensitivity/specificity
combination at those thresholds. The device implements those thresholds; it does not claim new clinical
performance.

## 3. Valid clinical association
Fast breathing at those thresholds is an established sign used in community case management of childhood
pneumonia (source above; WHO IMCI chart booklet to be appraised before any real submission — PRD §11).

## 4. Technical and analytical performance
Verification tests show the implementation matches the thresholds at every boundary (SRS-002, SRS-003) and refuses
out-of-scope ages, implausible rates and incomplete counts (RC-002, RC-003, RC-005).

## 5. Clinical performance
Not established for this fictional device. A real manufacturer would need clinical performance data in the
intended setting (CHWs, outdoor/low-light use), e.g. agreement with clinician-counted rate.

## 6. Conclusion
Illustrative only. Section 5 is an open gap that would block a real submission.
