# Code of conduct

Threadline brings together engineers, quality and regulatory professionals, clinicians and reviewers. People arrive
knowing a lot about one of those worlds and little about the others. Be patient with that.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Explain jargon when someone asks; nobody knows every standard.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, in any project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the project maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/JFrusher), or use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code.
