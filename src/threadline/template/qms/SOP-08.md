# SOP-08 Management Review

**Purpose.** Review the QMS for suitability, adequacy and effectiveness (ISO 13485:2016 §5.6).

## Procedure
1. Top management holds a review at least yearly.
2. Inputs (§5.6.2): feedback, complaints, reporting to regulators, audits, process monitoring, product monitoring,
   CAPA, follow-up of previous reviews, changes affecting the QMS, recommendations, new regulatory requirements.
   `threadline check` output and open findings are part of process monitoring.
3. Outputs (§5.6.3): improvements, product changes, regulatory changes, resource needs.
4. Record as a `management_review` item; actions become CAPA or change requests.
